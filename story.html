Our Story

Our school first opened its doors in Durban during 2023. Sarah Smith started this project so that everyone who loves pets has a place to learn how to take care of them the right way. We offer 12-week professional courses and quick 6-week skills classes.

We believe every pet owner should know the basics, like what to feed their pet or what to do in an emergency. Our courses also help students find new jobs in the pet world, or start their own pet business.
